<p>Rita and Skippy walk through the Meat Puppet Elimination Protocol: what it is, why it has that name, and how a text file solved a problem every AI operator hits.</p>

<p>Every AI session is stateless.  It wakes up knowing nothing.  Before MEP, Pierre was the message relay | finishing on one machine, reading the screen, walking to another, and retyping the context by hand so the next session knew what had happened.  A person being used as a serial cable.</p>

<p><strong>In this episode:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Why the handoff file has exactly three sections, and why a context dump is not context</li>
<li>The section everybody skips, and why it matters most: writing down what you could not verify</li>
<li>How it grew from one machine to cross ecosystem transfer and read only peer surfaces</li>
<li>The pidgin nobody designed.  Compressed phrases that emerged independently across sessions with no shared memory, and stabilised</li>
<li>The minimum viable version you can build on Monday</li>
</ul>
