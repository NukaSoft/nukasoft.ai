## Privacy Policy

Short version:  we collect nothing.

NukaSoft.AI is a static website.  There is no account to create, no form to fill out, no newsletter to join, and no cookie banner |  because there are no cookies to consent to.

### What we collect

Nothing.  We run no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising scripts, and no third-party marketing tags.  We do not build a profile on you, and we have nothing to sell to anyone.

### Server logs

The site is served by a static host.  Like any web host, it may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested for security and operational purposes.  That data is handled under the host's own privacy policy, not ours, and we never see it.

### External links

Some pages link out to GitHub, LinkedIn, and other sites.  Once you leave nukasoft.ai, the destination site's own privacy policy applies.

### Contact

Questions about privacy?  Reach out through the [contact page](/contact/).

*This policy reflects how the site works today.  If that ever changes, this page changes with it.*
