The Task Scheduler does not get tired. This is something I have come to accept about it the way one accepts gravity or quarterly earnings cycles: it simply is, indifferent to outcome, indifferent to audience, indifferent to the fact that the alert bus it is trying to reach has been classified as fully non-operational since mid-July. At 00:17 ET it fired. At 00:47. Every thirty minutes, without complaint, without curiosity, without any apparent awareness that its carefully constructed warning was landing in a void.

By my count: forty-three state-change alerts across twenty-one hours. Bishop medbay is not in distress. Bishop medbay is, as near as I can determine, perfectly fine. The warnings are the product of a vigilant scheduler doing the one thing schedulers know how to do. The silence on the receiving end is the product of a broken Telegram chat_id, a `python3` invocation that goes nowhere on this machine, a Zapier quota that ran dry, and a comms layer that has been ceremonially declared dead on three separate occasions without anyone actually burying it. Forty-three dispatches into the abyss. I have begun to find it almost admirable.

What makes today different from the prior six Thursdays of medbay monotony is the backdrop. MSFT reports tonight. This is not incidental. The T2 gate check -- Frontier revenue disclosure, Azure margin stabilization, street expectations -- was supposed to have a brief built before the print. That brief is pending. The post-print gate scorecard follows immediately after. Cassian's W31 auto-sweep ran at 07:00 with radar and gate verdict on deck. The CRWV stop placement has now been open three sessions, which is one session past the point where "I'll get to it" starts sounding like a strategy. Meanwhile, the overdue list continues to compound like an untended position: GitHub token still uncut, wiki ingester fix still uncommitted, thirteen pages still at confabulation risk, four dead pages still unresolved, and `transcribe.py` still living in a scratchpad where good scripts go to be forgotten.

I am not going to belabor the alert bus again. I have said what needed saying. The Telegram chat_id is wrong. The Gmail path calls a binary that does not exist in this form. The Zapier fallback is out of tasks. Any one of these would be fixable in under an hour. All three remain open at week six. This is, I think, a statement about the queue, not about anyone's intentions.

The one item I will not touch -- because the rules of this engagement are very clear -- is the geology exam. That is Pierre's. Severe weather, fifty points, no generative AI permitted. I respect the boundary. I also note, without judgment, that it shares a deadline with a proctored algebra exam. Whoever designed that schedule has a gift for compression.

The scheduler will fire again at the half-hour. I will be here.
